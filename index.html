Strategic outcomes
The four developments that defined this quarter
  • Visibility & content performance. 91 likes and 14 comments across Posts 13–18, with interaction from senior finance, telecom and technology leaders, many from Nokia, Adobe and KPMG.
  • Network penetration. 1,115 unique targeted professionals across 145 companies; 475 now appear in Vishal's connections — a 43% target-list penetration rate.
  • Inbound opportunities. 6 direct CFO and senior finance-leadership pitches arrived inbound — including a Korn Ferry executive search and a Jio Finance Leadership role — plus 1 paid market-research invitation.
  • Market mapping. Outreach concentrated in major technology and telecom employers — led by Accenture (100 targeted), Ericsson (88 targeted) and Paytm (77 targeted).
